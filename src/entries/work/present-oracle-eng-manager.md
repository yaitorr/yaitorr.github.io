Lead a cross-functional scrum team of full-stack JavaScript developers responsible for evolving Opower's Product Configuration Platform, which powers localized content and behavior management for multi-channel communications (Email, SMS, Push, IVR) at enterprise scale. The platform also enables Opower's JAMStack-based energy efficiency solutions, delivering insights to utility companies across the continental US and internationally. Partner with product managers and business stakeholders to prioritize feature development using agile methodologies, including Kanban and Shape Up frameworks. Maintain hands-on technical involvement through code reviews and pull request contributions while fostering team growth through mentorship and coaching. Focus on developing team autonomy and self-management capabilities to drive continuous improvement and delivery excellence.
